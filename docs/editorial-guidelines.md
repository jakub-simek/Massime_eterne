# Editorial Guidelines

## Documentation Language

All project documentation, README files, and code comments must be written in English. Edition texts, source transcriptions, and translation content may remain in their source or target languages.

## Base Text

The digital edition uses the freely accessible IntraText version of *Massime eterne* as its initial Italian base text:

`https://www.intratext.com/ixt/itasa0000/_IDX084.HTM`

IntraText documents the printed basis as: S. Alfonso Maria de Liguori, *OPERE ASCETICHE*, Vol. IX, pp. 381-395, Edizioni di Storia e Letteratura, Roma 1965.

## Text Layers

- **Base text**: Italian IntraText text.
- **Translation**: German working translation, section by section and traceable to the base text.
- **Meditation**: original devotional or reflective text based on a defined section.
- **Commentary**: philological, historical, or theological note.

## Editorial Intervention

- Obvious digital transcription errors are corrected only after checking the source.
- Meaning-bearing corrections are documented in a note.
- Orthography, punctuation, and historical forms are normally retained in the base text.
- Printed page numbers are preserved as `<pb>`.
- IntraText page boundaries are preserved as `<milestone unit="intratext-page">`.

## Translation And Meditation

- The translation should remain close to the text and must not be disguised as meditation.
- Meditation texts may be freer, but must name their textual basis.
- Each meditation should identify its section, language, status, and revision state.
